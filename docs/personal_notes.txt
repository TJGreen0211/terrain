Bake skybox
# ./build/cube.exe --bake-sky <name> [--sky-size <n>] [--sky-params <set>] [--sky-axes]
./build/cube.exe --bake-sky eve2 --sky-size 1024 --sky-params eve1 --sky-seed 7

To see a baked sky, launch the app with cube.exe --sky eve2, or set NEBULA_SKY_NAME in WorldConfig.h to make it the default.
